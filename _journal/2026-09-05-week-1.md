things are going really well. life is good.

the first weeks back to school always feel ludicrously slow. getting used to new schedules, unlocking new interiors across campus, meeting old friends again. this one has been no different.

my girlfriend just adopted the best and prettiest cat EVER. his name is stitch and he is already embracing the lap cat life. he is half russian blue and half clumsy.

i'm super excited for my research this semester at the microfluidics lab. i somehow have been given the control over a small CNC lab to clean and organize despite never having touched a CNC before. and i'm set to learn countless new flashy machines and doohickeys to help me make small rubbery straw squares. what a cool job.

i just saw sam o' nella at his book tour! my friend and i forged our way through a broken metro and hours of line just to get a silly signature and photo with him. i read a part of his book for the first time and it's beautifully weird. i can't wait to read the rest.

i met up with one of my friends and attempted to put together a song with some of her old lyrics. most of it was scrapped. many books and assorted items were fondled for foley purposes. it's fun making music again.

my roommates and i played bad piggies for 2 hours straight today. half a million dollars worth of education and we were still fighting for our lives to beat these levels. though we may have beat a world record.
