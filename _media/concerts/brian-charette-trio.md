okay seriously my mind was blown. this wasn't just jazz. it was a theatrical masterpiece. my jaw dropped like andrew neiman's dad at the end of whiplash. primarily because of the drummer Dano Šoltis, who had the most ridiculously nonchalant whimsy i've ever seen at a kit. i saw this in PRAGUE at venue on the VLTAVA RIVER and this still became one of my favorite moments on the trip.
