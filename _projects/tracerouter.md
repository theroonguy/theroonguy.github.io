<h2>Evolution of TRACEROUTER</h2>

TRACEROUTER initially began as a 1-month long game jam submission at the UMD Game Development Club's Fall 2025 Jam. The themes were "Speedrun" and "Virus," and I quickly took inspiration from an old Flash game called <i>Focus</i> and took its major concepts into a first-person, 3D parkour-platformer with movement inspired by <i>Ghostrunner</i> and visuals similar to <i>Super Hot</i>. The player could slide, run on walls, and slow time in a radius around them as they dodged virus missiles. It was a simple concept with a lot of focus on fine-tuning the movement and level design rather than content. We won 1st place in the jam, but by the end the feeling I felt most was that there was so much potential left to explore for the game.
