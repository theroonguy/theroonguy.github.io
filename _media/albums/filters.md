it's definitely a good album, and it's been growing on me. i really liked the opening with _unexpected_, and it really set the mood for the album. some of the slickest, pleasing-on-the-ears music i've listened to. feels like floating. my other favs are _multiphase_, _january_, and recently, _forget_. 

_temporary time_, his previous album, remains my favorite. but _filters_ shows up with a refreshingly soothing and feel-good vibe that i really dig.
