sooo honestly this show started out feeling like a 2. it was one of my last nights in sweden and i wanted to go out with one of my international friends before i left, so we weren't going to be picky. it was, by far, the Loudest Band. i had to wear my airpods. and when they say taiwanese psychedelic noise, they mean it. i could barely hear the singer and that man was Screaming.

but i will say, i actually got really into it by the end. it was like a nonstop action scene that somehow started out the gate at the final climax of a movie and ended 5 sequels in at 10 times the budget. they reeled it in in the middle of the set and showed they had dynamics. but yeah i can't say i've ever felt like moving at 100mph standing in one spot before.
